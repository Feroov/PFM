PFM - Personal Finance Manager

Version: 1.0.0

At FeroBYTE, our mission is to simplify personal finance management, helping you stay in control of your financial health. Our app is designed to give you powerful tools to track your finances, gain insights, and make informed decisions effortlessly.

Our Key Features:

  • Transaction Tracking: Categorize your income and expenses to get a detailed view of your finances.
  • Recurring Transactions: Automate your regular payments or income with recurring transactions.
  • Data Export: Easily export your transaction history to CSV, PDF, or Excel formats for further analysis or reporting.
  • Data Import: Seamlessly import transaction data from CSV, JSON, or Excel files, keeping all your financial information in one place.
  • Financial Insights: Visualize your data with interactive charts and reports to better understand your spending patterns.
  • Dark and Light Modes: Customize your app experience with light and dark mode themes for comfortable viewing.
  • Multi-Currency Support: Manage your transactions in different currencies, including USD, EUR, and GBP.
